# GoodSort PDF

**Convert Word documents, PowerPoint presentations and images into PDFs—individually, in batches or combined.**

**Free to use. No subscriptions. No locked features.**

**And best of all, it all runs on your PC—*offline*.** Your files stay on *your computer*, with no uploads to an online file conversion service. Convert, combine and compress even without an internet connection.

GoodSort PDF is a Windows PDF toolkit: a **jack of all trades for preparing PDFs**. It can prepare fortnightly school newsletters, turn a batch of mixed-format files into separate PDFs, combine an ordered set of documents into one PDF, or rearrange individual pages. Newsletters and Bulk convert support optional appended documents. Combine all uses one ordered list. All modes include automatic compression, supported hyperlink preservation and optional printing after saving.

## Get started

1. Choose **Newsletters**, **Bulk convert**, **Combine all** or **Arrange pages**.
2. Drop in your files. The first three modes also accept folders; Arrange pages opens one document at a time.
3. Check the order and names, then choose your **Save to** folder.
4. Optionally tick **Print after saving**. Click **GO**—if printing is selected, choose your printer and settings once for the batch.

The app saves the finished PDFs before sending them to the printer. See the [user guide](docs/USER-GUIDE.md) for step-by-step instructions.

## Made for everyday document jobs

- **Notices are optional.** Convert, compress and name newsletters on their own. Shared notices are always optional.
- **For schools and community groups.** Use year levels or your own groups, such as Joeys, Cubs and Scouts, with weekly, fortnightly or custom issue labels.
- **Works with existing PDFs.** Drop a folder into Bulk convert to compress and save them, with optional printing for the whole batch.
- **Ready to share.** Compression aims for about **10 MB per PDF**, with a flexible target for larger documents. Supported hyperlinks and colours are retained; images fit A4 without cropping.
- **Your originals stay untouched.** Outputs are saved as new PDFs, and existing output files are never overwritten.

## Requirements

- **64-bit Windows 10 version 2004 or later, or Windows 11 (x64).**
- **Desktop Microsoft Word** for Word/RTF files; **desktop Microsoft PowerPoint** for presentations. These apps are supplied separately.
- **No Office installation needed for PDFs and images.** No separate PDF reader or .NET installation is required.

See [supported formats](docs/USER-GUIDE.md#supported-formats) and [troubleshooting and limits](docs/USER-GUIDE.md#troubleshooting-and-limits) for details.

## How GoodSort PDF began

It started with the need to prepare fortnightly school newsletters: teaching teams supplied different document formats, and shared notices needed to follow each class newsletter. Version 1, **Newsletter Combiner**, turned that into consistently named PDFs ready to distribute.

Everyday use grew it into **GoodSort PDF**—adding compression, bulk conversion, combined documents, page arranging and batch printing. It is now useful for schools, clubs, community groups and anyone preparing documents to share, with the same straightforward approach: **free to use, offline, and no paid feature tiers.**
